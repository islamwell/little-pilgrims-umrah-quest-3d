Little Pilgrim's Umrah

A 3D First-Person Adventure of Sabr, Shukr & Forgiveness

🌟 What you will learn & practice:

🤍 Sincere Intention (Ikhlas): Starting solely for Allah's love.

🛡️ Sabr (Patience): Never getting angry, giving way to others in the crowd with a warm smile.

🎯 Khushu (Focus): 7 circuits of Tawaf with eyes and heart fixed on Allah.

🤲 Shukr (Gratitude): Tasting Zamzam & remembering Mother Hajar's reliance on Allah.

✨ Forgiveness: Cutting hair as a fresh clean start, forgiving everyone.

🎵 100% Halal: Pure Quran Recitation & Natural Audio. Strictly No Music.
🤍

Step 1: The Meeqat & Intention

Ihram, Talbiyah & The Sabr Shield

Chant the Talbiyah (Here I am, O Allah!):

لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ

"Labbayk Allahumma Labbayk, Labbayka La Shareeka Laka Labbayk..."

🛡️ Sabr Shield Challenge: Tap each test to activate patience!
🕋

Step 2: Tawaf (7 Circuits)

Counter-clockwise around the Ka'bah

0 / 7
🟢
The Green Light Starting Line:

Look for the glowing green beacon! Every circuit begins and ends directly aligned with the Black Stone (al-Hajar al-Aswad).

Dhikr for this Circuit:

Face the Ka’bah at the Green Light line to begin!

Start here! Face Ka'bah, raise right hand: Bismillahi Allahu Akbar!

🕌

Step 3: Maqam Ibrahim

Station of Prophet Ibrahim (peace be upon him)

After completing 7 circuits, we pray 2 gentle Rak'ahs of prayer behind Maqam Ibrahim, reciting Surah Al-Kafirun and Surah Al-Ikhlas with pure focus (Khushu).

💡 Did you know? This crystal shrine holds the stone that Prophet Ibrahim stood on while building the Ka'bah with his son Ismail!
💧

Drink Zamzam with Shukr (Thanks)

The Miraculous Spring of Makkah

Turn towards the Ka'bah, say Bismillah, drink in 3 calm sips with your right hand, and thank Allah for all His blessings!

The Prophet's Du'a for Zamzam:

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَافِعًا، وَرِزْقًا وَاسِعًا، وَشِفَاءً مِنْ كُلِّ دَاءٍ

"O Allah, I ask You for beneficial knowledge, abundant provision, and cure from every ailment."

⛰️

Step 4: Sa'i (Safa & Marwah)

Walking the Path of Mother Hajar

0 / 7 Laps
🌿 The Ultimate Lesson of Sabr:

Lady Hajar walked between these two hills with patience (Sabr) and complete trust in Allah (Tawakkul). Because she never gave up, Allah brought forth the Zamzam spring!

🏃‍♂️ Green Light Zone: Hasten and jog briskly when passing under the emerald lights!

Begin Lap 1 at Mount Safa! Face the Ka’bah and make sincere du'a.

✂️

Step 5: Taqsir & Forgiveness

Shedding Sins & Fresh Clean Heart

The final rite of Umrah! Boys trim their hair evenly, and girls trim a fingertip length. This represents humility before Allah and starting life fresh with all mistakes forgiven.

✨ The Prophet's Supplication:

"The Messenger of Allah prayed three times for mercy and forgiveness for those who cut their hair with humility!"
